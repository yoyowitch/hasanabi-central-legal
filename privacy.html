Privacy Policy

Hasanabi Central Uploader · Last updated November 15, 2026

Hasanabi Central Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used by the Hasanabi Central YouTube channel to send its own short videos to its own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what the app does with information from TikTok.

What we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives, with their permission:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar and an account identifier), used only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The account owner then reviews, edits and posts them in the TikTok app.

The app does not read your videos, followers, messages, likes or any other TikTok data.

How it is stored

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in a private settings file. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's. We do not sell, share or rent any information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Keeping and deleting it

You can disconnect at any time with the "Disconnect" button in the app, which revokes the app's access with TikTok and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by the channel's operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
